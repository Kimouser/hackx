# Pitch Deck Outline: Project Guardian

## Slide 1: Title Screen
- **Project Name**: Project Guardian
- **Tagline**: AI-Powered Safe-Passage & Civic Accountability.
- **Visual**: A glowing safe route on a dark map interface.

## Slide 2: The Problem
- **Narrative**: Traditional navigation apps are built for cars, efficiency, and speed. They are not built for human safety. 
- **Statistic**: Highlighting the fear and statistics of street harassment, poorly lit areas, and unresponsive municipal bodies.
- **Conclusion**: Women are forced to choose between the fastest route (often unsafe alleys) or guessing the safest route.

## Slide 3: The Solution (Safety First Engine)
- **Concept**: A navigation ecosystem that flips the paradigm: Safety over Speed.
- **Mechanism**: Showing how the algorithm layers lighting density, active business hours, and community data to carve out "Safe Paths".

## Slide 4: Real-Time Safe Spaces
- **Feature Focus**: One-touch access to 24/7 hotspots.
- **Visual**: The map instantly filtering out noise and highlighting only hospitals, police stations, and 24-hour medical shops in times of distress.

## Slide 5: Civic Accountability (The Municipal Loop)
- **The Concept**: Safety isn't just about avoiding bad areas; it's about fixing them.
- **Workflow**: User reports a broken streetlight -> Community Upvotes -> Automated escalating emails to the Municipal Corporation -> Volunteer amplification if ignored.

## Slide 6: The Emergency Ecosystem (Software + Hardware)
- **Software**: Volunteer Bodyguard network.
- **Hardware (Revenue Model)**: The Guardian Bracelet. A premium capacitive-touch wearable that triggers silent check-ups and escalates to police dispatch if the user goes unresponsive. 

## Slide 7: Technical Architecture
- **Tech Stack**: React Native, Node.js, MongoDB, Mapbox.
- **Highlight**: The custom routing weighting algorithm.

## Slide 8: What's Next & The Ask
- **Future phases**: Partnerships with physical volunteer groups, municipal API integrations, hardware prototyping.
- **Closing statement**: "Empowering communities to reclaim the night."
